# Reader

Tap any chapter to open it in the Reader. Tap the **center of the screen** at any time to show or hide the controls.

## Reading modes

Two modes, switchable in Theme & Settings → **Direction**:

- **Paginated (horizontal)**: pages turn like a book.
  - **Swipe**: drag pages left/right
  - **Tap**: tap the left/right edges
- **Scroll (vertical)**: continuous scroll. The next chapter auto-loads when you reach the end of the current one.

The page counter at the top right tracks your position in either mode (e.g., "3 / 142").

## Controls

When the controls are visible (tap the center to toggle):

| Position     | Control         |
| ------------ | --------------- |
| Top left     | Back            |
| Top right    | Chapter counter |
| Bottom left  | Progress bar    |
| Bottom right | Menu            |

The menu contains:

- **Previous / Next chapter**
- **Go to chapter** (picker)
- **Bookmarks**: list of saved positions
- **Theme & Settings**: fonts, themes, customization
- **Search in chapter**: find within the current chapter

## Themes

Six built-in reader themes: **White / Sepia / Gray / Slate / Dark / Black**.

The active theme controls the page background, text color, and accent. Change it in **Theme & Settings → Theme**.

### Custom themes

Tap **+ Custom** in the theme picker to build your own, pick background, text, and accent colors. Saved themes appear alongside the built-ins and can be edited or deleted later.

### Sharing themes

Tap **Share** on a custom theme to export it as a `.glyphtheme` file. Anyone can import it by opening the file in Glyph, that lets people share reading themes the same way they share music playlists.

## Fonts & typography

Five fonts out of the box: **System, Georgia, Palatino, Charter, Times New Roman**.

In **Theme & Settings** you can also adjust:

- Font size
- Bold toggle
- Line spacing, letter spacing, word spacing
- Text alignment, Left, Center, Justified
- Horizontal padding (margins)

## Brightness & screen behavior

- **Brightness slider** at the top of Theme & Settings, independent of your system brightness, so you can dim the Reader without affecting the rest of iOS.
- **Keep screen on**: prevents auto-lock while reading. Useful for long sessions.

## Pre-fetch

In **Settings → Reader → Pre-fetch**, choose how many upcoming chapters Glyph loads in the background (0 to 5). Higher values mean smoother continuous-scroll reading but more network use. Default is 1.

## Bookmarks

Mark a position so you can return to it later, useful for a favorite passage, a tricky scene, or just a placeholder.

- **Add a bookmark**: menu → **Add Bookmark**. A small accent indicator appears on the left edge of the page.
- **Add a note**: tap an existing bookmark in the list to add or edit a note.
- **Jump to a bookmark**: menu → **Bookmarks** → tap any entry.
- **Delete a bookmark**: swipe left on the entry.

Bookmarks sync across devices via iCloud.

## Search in chapter

Find any phrase in the current chapter:

1. Menu → **Search in Chapter**
2. Type your query, matches highlight as you type
3. Use the arrows at the bottom to jump between matches
4. The counter shows your position (e.g., "3 / 15")

Matches are highlighted with the reader theme's accent color.
