The waitlist is open. One email address, one message when a build is ready, and
nothing else — no newsletter, no launch sequence, no reminder that you have not
opened the app this week.

## What the waitlist is for

Apple caps how many people can read through TestFlight. The cap is the reason
a list exists at all; it is not a mailing list and it is not a funnel.

- Invitations go out in the order addresses arrived.
- Each wave is small, because a build that breaks should break for a few people.
- If a wave fills, the rest of the list rolls into the next one.

## What happens after you get in

You will receive the TestFlight link, install the app, and add a source. Glyph
ships without any, which surprises people in a good way and a bad way — the
app is empty until you decide what to read.

> TestFlight builds expire after ninety days. When yours does, the next one is
> waiting; the app keeps your library either way.

## What is not decided yet

Pricing is settled: it is free. Everything else about the App Store release is
not, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest. If Apple pulls the app,
sideloading and the open source format are the fallback, and the sources do not
stop working because the app left a store.
